Dharma — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

Dharma is a Hindu learning companion. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. We designed the app to collect as little as possible and to never sell your data or show ads.

No sign-up required

You can use Dharma without creating an account or giving us your name or email. When you first open the app, we create an anonymous account for you automatically. It has no personal identifiers — it exists only so your progress can be backed up and restored on your device.

What we store, and where

  • On your device: your reading progress, reflections, notes, preferences, and any profile photo you add. This is the primary copy of your data.
  • Backed up to our servers (Supabase): your learning progress and reflections are synced to your anonymous account so you don't lose them if you reinstall or change devices. This data is stored encrypted at rest and is accessible only to your account. Your profile photo stays on your device and is not uploaded.

The "Ask Krishna" AI feature

When you send a message to the AI companion, that message — together with limited context you have provided (such as your first name, your stated interests and familiarity, and reflections relevant to the conversation) — is sent to Google's Gemini AI so it can generate a reply. This happens through our secure server, which holds the AI key; the app itself never contains it.

We do not store your chat messages on our servers, and our server does not keep a copy of the context it forwards. Google processes the request under its own terms; see the Google Gemini API terms. The AI is a reflection tool inspired by Krishna's teachings — it is not a real person and not a substitute for professional advice.

Analytics

  • Usage analytics (PostHog): we collect anonymous, aggregated usage events — for example, that a lesson was completed or a screen was opened — to understand how the app is used and improve it. These events are tied to a random device identifier, not to your name or your anonymous account.
  • Crash reports (Sentry): if the app crashes, we receive a technical diagnostic report so we can fix the problem.
  • A separate copy of basic usage counts is also kept only on your device.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders (a daily verse, festival dates, and gentle nudges) are scheduled locally on your device. We do not operate a push-notification server and we do not track whether you open them.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not ask for your contacts, location, or precise identity.

Your choices

  • Export a backup: in the app, open You → Account & data to export all your data as a file.
  • Delete everything: the same screen lets you permanently delete your account and all associated data — on your device and on our servers. This cannot be undone.

Children

Dharma is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
