# User interview guide — beta rounds (Aug–Sep 2026)

Workstream 3 of `docs/diwali-launch-plan.md`. 30–40 minutes, video call or in person, within a week of the tester having used the app. Target: 8–10 interviews in round 1 (Aug 18–29). Record with permission; pull verbatim quotes for the launch narrative (get explicit OK before using any).

**Ground rules for the interviewer (you):** ask about their life before the app; never demo or defend; "tell me about the last time…" beats "would you…"; silence is your friend — let them fill it; chase specifics ("what did you do next?") not opinions.

## 1. Their relationship with the tradition (10 min) — the segment check

- Growing up, what did Hinduism look like in your house? Who carried it?
- These days, when does the tradition show up in your life? Walk me through the last time.
- Is there a moment you felt you *should* know something — a festival, a ritual, a question from a kid or a friend — and didn't? What did you do?
- Before Dharma, where did you go for this? (Google, YouTube, family WhatsApp, a book?) What was wrong with that?

## 2. Their actual usage (10 min) — behavior, not opinion

- Open the app right now — where are you in it? Show me around what you've done.
- When did you last open it, and what made you open it? (Notification? Habit? A specific question?)
- What's the thing you did most? What did you try once and never again — and why?
- Did you ever hit something that felt wrong, off, or preachy? Where?
- (If they used Ask Krishna) Read me a question you asked. Did the answer land?

## 3. Value and the PMF question (10 min)

- If Dharma disappeared tomorrow, what would you actually miss? (Nothing is a fine answer — chase it.)
- **Sean Ellis, verbally:** How would you feel — very disappointed, somewhat disappointed, or not disappointed? Why?
- Have you told anyone about it? What did you say, word for word?
- Who do you know who needs this more than you? What stops you from sending it to them today?
- What would make this a no-brainer to recommend to [that person]?

## 4. Trust and money (5 min)

- The content claims to be citation-backed and expert-reviewed. Did you notice? Does it change anything for you?
- If the app stays free and asks for support once a year — would you? What amount feels right? What would feel gross?
